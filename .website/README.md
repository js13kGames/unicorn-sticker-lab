# Unicorn Sticker Lab

You're running a tiny sticker studio. Drag magical bits and pieces onto
the canvas, overlap a couple of them, and tap **Sticker it!** to see
what you made. Some combinations are secret recipes and those come with
a name, a little chime, and a proper fanfare. Everything else still
prints as a real sticker, just an unnamed one of your own invention.
Nothing you make ever gets thrown away, so you can hoard all the cuteness (until you run out of space).

**Controls:** tap or click, that's it. Tap a piece from the tray to
drop it on the canvas, drag it where you want, tap **Sticker it!** to combine. 

Not sure what to try next? The banner up by the studio mascot (watch for rainbow plops) gives
you a nudge, like "make something that sparkles", without
spoiling the exact answer, since usually more than one combo will do
the trick. 

Everything you've discovered lives in your **Collection**,
with riddle hints for the recipes still hiding from you. Every sticker
you've ever printed, named or not, ends up in your **Album**. Tap one
to save it as a PNG or clear it out if you don't want it around.

Keep discovering recipes and new pieces and effects unlock
along the way: balloons, sparkle, hearts, that sort of thing. Find all 16
and you'll earn a bonus piece: a googly eye for all your googly needs!
